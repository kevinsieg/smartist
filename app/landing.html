Features

sets

Setlists

Generate by energy, duration, and feel. Drag-and-drop reorder. Save to gigs. Share as PDF.

library

Songs

Full catalogue with play count, AI lyrics, audio and sheet music attachments, full change log.

rights

PRO

Import performing rights organisation data — CSV with dry-run preview and catalogue auto-matching.

gigs

Gig management

Venues, contracts, set times, contacts, and finances — linked to your setlists.

venues

Venue database

Searchable profiles with capacity, genre fit, booking contacts, and fee history.

AI

Tour planning

AI-optimised routing, booking outreach, revenue forecast, conflict detection.

insights

Artist Hub

Song availability across platforms, social media reach, physical sales, and streaming integrations.

live

Stage view

Full-screen setlist display built for the stage. Song, key, capo, and notes — nothing else.
